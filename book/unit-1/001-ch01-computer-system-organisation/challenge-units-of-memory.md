# More practice on units of memory, and 1000 or 1024?

**Challenge** · harder practice · Chapter 1, section 1.7

## 1000 or 1024?

- In memory, sizes come in powers of 2, so **1 KB = 2¹⁰ = 1024 bytes**. **CBSE answers use 1024.**
- Disk makers and the metric (SI) system use powers of 10: 1 kB = 1000 bytes, 1 GB = 1 000 000 000 bytes.
- To avoid confusion, some books write **KiB, MiB, GiB** (kibibyte, mebibyte, gibibyte) for the 1024 units.
- That is why a disk sold as "1 TB" (10¹² bytes) shows about 931 GB in your computer: 10¹² ÷ 1024³ ≈ 931.

## Practice

**Q1.** 6 MB = ? bits.
**Answer.** 6 × 1024 × 1024 × 8 = 50 331 648 bits.

**Q2.** 49 152 KB = ? MB.
**Answer.** 49 152 ÷ 1024 = 48 MB.

**Q3.** Arrange in descending order: 1.5 GB, 1600 MB, 2²¹ KB, 1 572 864 KB.
**Answer.** In MB: 1.5 GB = 1536 MB; 1600 MB; 2²¹ KB = 2048 MB; 1 572 864 KB = 1536 MB.
Order: 2²¹ KB > 1600 MB > 1.5 GB = 1 572 864 KB.

**Q4.** A phone has 128 GB of storage. The apps and the system use 40 GB. How many 4 MB songs fit in the rest?
**Answer.** 128 − 40 = 88 GB = 88 × 1024 MB = 90 112 MB; 90 112 ÷ 4 = 22 528 songs.

**Q5.** Which is larger: 2⁴⁰ bytes or 1000 GB?
**Answer.** 2⁴⁰ bytes = 1 TB = 1024 GB, so 2⁴⁰ bytes is larger.

**Q6.** A file of 3 MB is sent over a 24 Mbps connection. About how long does it take?
**Answer.** 3 MB = 3 × 8 = 24 megabits (taking 1 MB ≈ 8 Mb); 24 ÷ 24 = about 1 second.

**Q7.** How many nibbles are there in 1 KB?
**Answer.** 1 KB = 8192 bits; 8192 ÷ 4 = 2048 nibbles.
