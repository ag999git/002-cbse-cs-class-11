# Chapter 1: Computer System Organisation: Fill in the blanks (25)

*Chapter 1 · Questions (online). Every question is followed by its answer and the reason. The section of the book is given in brackets after the difficulty (E easy, M medium, H hard).*

**Q1.** Data that has been processed so that it means something is called ________. *(E · 1.1)*

**Answer.** information. *Why:* data is raw; processing turns it into information, as raw milk and tea leaves become chai.

**Q2.** The parts of a computer you can see and touch are called ________. *(E · 1.2)*

**Answer.** hardware.

**Q3.** The programs that tell the hardware what to do are called ________. *(E · 1.2)*

**Answer.** software.

**Q4.** A device connected to the computer from outside, such as a printer, is called a ________. *(M · 1.3)*

**Answer.** peripheral. *Why:* "peripheral" means "on the edge", outside the main box.

**Q5.** OMR stands for ________. *(E · 1.3)*

**Answer.** Optical Mark Reader.

**Q6.** A dot-matrix printer is an ________ printer. *(E · 1.3)*

**Answer.** impact. *Why:* its pins strike an inked ribbon against the paper.

**Q7.** Output shown on a screen is called ________ copy. *(E · 1.3)*

**Answer.** soft.

**Q8.** A ________ draws large drawings, such as building plans, with pens on big sheets of paper. *(M · 1.3)*

**Answer.** plotter.

**Q9.** CPU stands for ________. *(E · 1.4)*

**Answer.** Central Processing Unit.

**Q10.** ALU stands for ________. *(E · 1.4)*

**Answer.** Arithmetic Logic Unit.

**Q11.** The tiny, very fast stores inside the CPU are called ________. *(E · 1.4)*

**Answer.** registers.

**Q12.** The CPU repeats three steps for every instruction: fetch, ________ and execute. *(E · 1.4)*

**Answer.** decode. *Why:* after the instruction is fetched, the control unit works out what it means.

**Q13.** The main circuit board of a computer is the ________. *(E · 1.4)*

**Answer.** motherboard.

**Q14.** RAM stands for ________. *(E · 1.6)*

**Answer.** Random Access Memory. *Why:* "random access" means any location can be reached directly, in the same time.

**Q15.** Memory that loses its contents when the power goes off is called ________. *(E · 1.6)*

**Answer.** volatile. *Why:* like petrol, it "evaporates" when left alone (without power).

**Q16.** Starting a computer, when the start-up program checks the hardware and loads the operating system, is called ________. *(E · 1.6)*

**Answer.** booting.

**Q17.** When the data the CPU needs is found in the cache, it is called a cache ________. *(M · 1.6)*

**Answer.** hit. *Why:* when it is not found, it is a cache miss and the data must come from RAM.

**Q18.** The slice of a track on a disk is called a ________. *(M · 1.6)*

**Answer.** sector.

**Q19.** EEPROM stands for ________. *(H · 1.6)*

**Answer.** Electrically Erasable Programmable Read Only Memory.

**Q20.** Pen drives and SSDs use ________ memory. *(E · 1.6)*

**Answer.** flash.

**Q21.** The smallest unit of data is a ________. *(E · 1.7)*

**Answer.** bit. *Why:* a single 0 or 1 (binary digit).

**Q22.** 1 byte = ________ bits. *(E · 1.7)*

**Answer.** 8.

**Q23.** 1 TB = ________ GB. *(E · 1.7)*

**Answer.** 1024.

**Q24.** 1 PB = 2 to the power ________ bytes. *(H · 1.7)*

**Answer.** 50. *Why:* KB = 2¹⁰, MB = 2²⁰, GB = 2³⁰, TB = 2⁴⁰, PB = 2⁵⁰ bytes.

**Q25.** To change a big unit into a smaller one, you ________. *(E · 1.7)*

**Answer.** multiply. *Why:* ₹5 = 500 paise: one big unit breaks into many small ones.
