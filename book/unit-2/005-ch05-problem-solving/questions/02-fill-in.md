## Fill in the blanks

**Q1.** The five steps of problem solving end with test and ______. *(E · 5.2)*

**Answer.** debug. *Why:* after delivery comes maintenance.

**Q2.** A mistake in a program is called a ______. *(E · 5.2)*

**Answer.** bug.

**Q3.** Testing finds ______ there is a fault; debugging finds where and why. *(E · 5.2)*

**Answer.** whether.

**Q4.** Testing the joined parts of a program together is ______ testing. *(E · 5.2)*

**Answer.** integration.

**Q5.** Test values just inside and just outside a limit are called ______ values. *(E · 5.2)*

**Answer.** boundary.

**Q6.** An algorithm must always ______ after a finite number of steps. *(E · 5.3)*

**Answer.** stop (end). *Why:* finiteness.

**Q7.** Every step of an algorithm must be exact: this is called ______. *(E · 5.3)*

**Answer.** precision (definiteness).

**Q8.** An algorithm has zero or more inputs and ______ or more outputs. *(M · 5.3)*

**Answer.** one.

**Q9.** GCD stands for ______. *(E · 5.3)*

**Answer.** greatest common divisor (also called HCF).

**Q10.** Euclid divides the last divisor by the last ______ until it is 0. *(E · 5.3)*

**Answer.** remainder.

**Q11.** LCM = a × b ÷ ______. *(M · 5.3)*

**Answer.** GCD. *Check:* 30 × 21 ÷ 3 = 210.

**Q12.** The GCD of 48 and 18 by Euclid is ______. *(M · 5.3)*

**Answer.** 6: 48 ÷ 18 r 12; 18 ÷ 12 r 6; 12 ÷ 6 r 0.

**Q13.** The flowchart symbol for Start and Stop is the ______. *(E · 5.4)*

**Answer.** terminal (a rounded rectangle; an oval is also accepted).

**Q14.** A decision box has one way in and ______ ways out. *(E · 5.4)*

**Answer.** two (Yes and No).

**Q15.** The three building blocks of every algorithm are sequence, selection and ______. *(E · 5.4)*

**Answer.** repetition (iteration, loop).

**Q16.** The flowchart symbols come from the standard ISO ______. *(H · 5.4)*

**Answer.** 5807.

**Q17.** Arrows in a flowchart are called ______ lines. *(E · 5.4)*

**Answer.** flow.

**Q18.** The condition "age is at least 18" is written age ______ 18. *(E · 5.4)*

**Answer.** ≥.

**Q19.** Two conditions that must BOTH be true are joined with ______. *(E · 5.4)*

**Answer.** AND.

**Q20.** The five-step method is Read, Mark, List, Draw, ______. *(E · 5.5)*

**Answer.** Check.

**Q21.** In W6 a student fails if the ______ mark is below 33. *(M · 5.5)*

**Answer.** lowest. *Why:* if the lowest passes, every mark passes.

**Q22.** In a ladder of decisions, the ______ Yes decides the answer. *(M · 5.5)*

**Answer.** first.

**Q23.** The keyword ______ shows a result in pseudocode. *(E · 5.6)*

**Answer.** PRINT.

**Q24.** Every IF in pseudocode is closed with ______. *(E · 5.6)*

**Answer.** END IF.

**Q25.** "total ← 0" means the same as SET total ______ 0. *(E · 5.6)*

**Answer.** =.

**Q26.** Pseudo means ______. *(E · 5.6)*

**Answer.** false (not real).

**Q27.** Following an algorithm by hand with sample inputs is a ______ run. *(E · 5.7)*

**Answer.** dry.

**Q28.** A ______ table records the value of every variable during a dry run. *(E · 5.7)*

**Answer.** trace.

**Q29.** The time adder gave 6 h 70 min because it forgot that 60 minutes make one ______. *(E · 5.7)*

**Answer.** hour.

**Q30.** Halving finds a name among 100 sorted contacts in at most ______ looks. *(M · 5.8)*

**Answer.** 7.

**Q31.** How the number of steps grows with the input is ______ complexity. *(E · 5.8)*

**Answer.** time.

**Q32.** How much memory an algorithm needs is its ______ complexity. *(E · 5.8)*

**Answer.** space.

**Q33.** Breaking a problem into parts is called ______. *(E · 5.9)*

**Answer.** decomposition.

**Q34.** A tree showing the parts of a system is a ______ diagram. *(E · 5.9)*

**Answer.** structure.

**Q35.** Keeping only what matters and hiding the rest is ______. *(M · 5.9)*

**Answer.** abstraction.
